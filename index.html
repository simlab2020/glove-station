Glove Sizing — simulation lab

Find your glove size

Lay your hand flat on the screen, fingers together, thumb tucked. Put the little-finger edge against the purple bar, level with your knuckles. Whichever band your index knuckle lands in is your size — tap it.

Hand scan

Lay your whole hand flat — fingers together, thumb tucked in, and press the heel of your palm down too. Hold still. Do it three times so the station can see whether it gets the same answer twice.

Lay your hand flat and slide the bars in until they touch each side at the knuckles. Drag with your other hand.
